# 2026-10-19: the frogs that die upside down

## What happened

Dominic looked at a battle screenshot (the website's battle picture) and said the dying frogs "look upside
down". They did: since D-106 a hopper's fall is one last hop that turns the whole baked sprite 180 degrees
(`visual.rotation = -PI`), meant as "lands on its back". On a flat pixel sprite that is just the frog drawn
upside down, shield, spear and banner too. The website picture was retaken first to avoid them; this round
fixes the fall in the game.

Only one body takes this path: creatures with `"gait": "hop"` in `data/creatures.json` (the frog), unmounted.
A frog on a newt falls the four-legged way (the newt's legs fold), crew members on an engine fall as hoppers too.

Claude (concept-first, D-080) put the fall behind a concept switch (`UnitView.concept.fall`, read by
`_hop_fall`) and drew round HF1 with the sheet tool (`tests/sheets/frog_fall_hf1.json`; the sheet tool gained
`concept` and `tip` on a unit ref):

- **A back**: today's fall, for the "before".
- **B side**: a short hop, then a topple onto its side the way `die_tip` says (the side combat_fx raises the
  dust on), and a little bounce, like the biped fall.
- **C splat**: a short hop and a belly flop: squashed flat, legs flung out behind, a wobble.
- **D kick**: B, then the legs stretch out stiff and kick twice. At game scale the kick barely shows.

Viking frog spearmen (shield and banner) and swamp frog workers, five facings mid-fall (0.3 s) and landed
(1.2 s), the fall frame by frame in facing 1, and a frog on a newt to show it's untouched. Placement passes on
every row; run_tests and placement_check pass. Waiting on Dominic's pick; the game still plays A until then.
