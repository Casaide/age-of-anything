# 2026-11-13: The Greeks in their kit (#451)

## What happened

Dominic: "greeks next", the same way as the Romans. So the Greeks got a body of their own and a kit for each line by age,
from the approved concept sheet (human-redesign/greeks-foot.png).

- **The body** (`tools/vox/greek.lobster`, greek.vox): the Roman proportions, with dark curly hair, a full beard, a chiton
  in team colour with a white girdle, and sandals laced up the shin.
- **The kit** (`tools/vox/greek_gear.lobster`): a linen corslet with yokes and strips, the same faced with bronze scales,
  a muscle cuirass, a bronze pilos, a Thracian helmet with its forward peak, a closed Corinthian with a tall crest, an Attic
  helmet with a crest on a stand, a big dished hoplon with a white lambda, a white oval thureos, a wicker crescent, the
  Cretan archer's band, a tall fox-skin cap, and the priest's laurel wreath with a long grey beard and a mantle off one
  shoulder. The Lionheart hero wears the Roman lion hood (same head).
- **The shapes both cultures share** moved into `tools/vox/worn.lobster`; the Roman models came out byte for byte the same.
- **Kits** (families.json, hellenic): spearmen go from a wooden spear and pilos to dory, hoplon, Corinthian and cuirass;
  swordsmen from a club to bronze sword, thureos and Thracian, then xiphos, Attic and scale; archers wear the band;
  skirmishers javelins, pelte and fox cap; healers the wreath.

## Bugs on the way

- Kits dropped "flying greeks"' wings (3 of 5 flew). Wings are only there when asked, so now they always keep their slot.
- The new parts made norse frogs draw differently, and a movement test (`test_movement`, head-on) failed on the new mix.
  Kit-only parts now carry `only_if: ["greek"]` (D-250), and norse frogs are exactly as before.
- The priest's wreath, at 0 points, turned up on woodcutters and lancers: it's healers only now.

## Later: the cavalry's shield (#455)

Dominic sent a picture straight after the Greeks merged (`images/2026-11-13/09-cavalry-scutum-through-horse.png`): the Roman
cavalryman's scutum cut through him and his horse. The generator had given the lancer the foot soldier's tall curved
shield, and a seated rider holds it where a standing man does, so its lower half ran down into the horse.
`rider_check` now measures it: of a held shield's voxels, how many lie inside the mount. The scutum came out at 17 to 23%,
the oval scutum and the thureos close behind; a round shield, the hoplon and the pelte stay under 5%.

The Roman riders now wear their sheet: the heavy cavalryman has mail, the galea, a lance and a new flat oval (`shield.parma`,
`roman_parma.vox`, 0% inside the horse); the scout wears the sun hat and no armour; the horse archer the cone helmet and
mail. The tall shields are marked `foot` in gear.json, and rider_check fails if any culture's mounted kit carries one.
(`images/2026-11-13/10-roman-cavalry-kit.png`.) Greek riders wait: their kits moved the movement tests on the centaurs.
The rider-legs fix on Creel's main needs the pin moved, which is its own piece.
