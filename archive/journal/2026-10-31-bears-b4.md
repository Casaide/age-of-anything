# 2026-10-31: bears B4, one mass instead of balls, and the lump behind the neck

Dominic: "the bears also look too much like made of spheres and have a weird bit out the back of their neck".

## What happened

- **The lump was a bug.** A biped bear's neck is a rod from the withers to the head. The head socket leans forward
  with the torso (8 to 11 degrees), but the rod's top end didn't: it stopped where the head would be if the bear
  stood straight, about 2 units behind the skull. Its rounded end stuck out behind the head. It was plainest
  from the back (f4 to f6) and on the giants. It came over from the Godot kit as it was. Fixed in `kit_torso`
  (`src/model/kit_bodies.lobster`); the brown bear's shoulder hump, a separate ball just behind the neck, made the
  rest of the shelf there. Picture 05 is the worker before the fix, with and without the hump.
- **Round B4** (`tests/sheets/bears_b4.json`, `docs/concepts/bears/README.md`): a new kit option, `body.loft`,
  builds a biped's torso as one mesh. It is sliced level by level through the old volumes, the neck and the
  shoulders, smoothed so they blend, with a ring laid through each level. Three options on today's bears: A
  smooth, B carved into flat-shaded planes, C smooth with limbs tapered from the root. Picture 04 is the
  close-up. Nothing is in play: today's bears only have the neck fix (BAKE_VERSION 9).
- **"overlap".** Dominic caught B's body through its cuirass in patches (picture 06). The loft is fuller than the
  chest ball the armour was fitted to; a lofted bear's armour is now fitted to the loft (`torso_fit`). The
  placement check missed it: armour and its body may share volume. Claude tried a rule (patches of body inside
  the armour's outline, seen from each bake camera). It caught B at 7 patches, but rats and bears already in play
  reached 5 to 7, so it can't tell them apart yet. It went to the backlog, not the check.
- The black bear's banner had been resting on the stray neck end; with that gone it floated 2.1 units off the
  back (the check caught it). Its socket moved in.

## Decisions

- D-189: Dominic picked **A** (the smooth loft) and confirmed the lump is gone on it ("lump is gone on A"). Claude had
  recommended C. A is in play for the brown, black and polar bears (BAKE_VERSION 10); the mount bear keeps its
  volumes. Putting it in play, the sheet caught the age-2 slinger's hand at ease cutting into the fuller shoulder:
  the loft's shoulders now take 0.9 of the arm's top radius (1.05 before), and the hand clears. The full check then
  caught four skirmisher lines and a black-bear hero with the hand at ease in the cuirass: fitting the armour to
  the loft had taken in the shoulders' width too. The fit now measures the loft without its shoulders.
