# 2026-11-13: The Royal kit

## What happened
- Claude built the third culture from the approved sheets (`human-redesign/royal-foot.png`, `royal-riders.png`), as the Greeks were
  built (#462): a Royal body (`royal.vox`, from `tools/vox/royal.lobster`) on the Roman build, with bowl-cut brown hair, a
  knee-length team tunic with long sleeves, a belt with a strap end, dark hose and turnshoes.
- Worn gear in `tools/vox/royal_gear.lobster`: a quilted gambeson (armour.leather), a mail hauberk (armour.mail), mail under a team
  surcoat with a gold cross (armour.surcoat), an iron kettle hat (helm.cap), a nasal helmet over a coif (helm.nasal), the flat-topped
  great helm (helm.iron), the archer's team hood, the slinger's mail coif, the monk's habit and cowl, and a heater shield.
- New parts, all `only_if: ["royal"]` so no other race's draws move: shield.heater, helm.nasal, hood.coif, hat.hood, hat.cowl
  (healers only), armour.surcoat, weapon.sword.arming, weapon.bow.long. Their stats copy the parts they stand in for.
- Royal kits per line and age in `families.json`. helm.iron and armour.mail got their own gear entries; other bodies keep the
  fits they had (helm.iron keeps the cap's Greek and Roman fits), so no other culture's look changed.
- A new material `habit` (#3a2e26) for the monk.

## Decisions
- The kite shield's held model would have changed every race's kite, so the Royal lines carry the heater instead; a new kite
  model can come with its own look check later.
- The longbow and the arming sword draw with the shared bow and sword gear for now.
