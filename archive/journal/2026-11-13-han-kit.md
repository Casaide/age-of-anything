# 2026-11-13: The Han kit

## What happened
- Claude built the sixth culture from the approved sheets (`human-redesign/han-base.png`, `han-foot.png`, `han-riders.png`)
  as the cultures before it (#469). The Han body (`han.vox`, from `tools/vox/han.lobster`) is on the Roman build: hair drawn
  up into a topknot tied with white cloth, a knee-length team robe wrapped right over left with a black edge, a black girdle
  with a gold hook, light trousers wrapped below the knee, black shoes.
- Gear in `tools/vox/han_gear.lobster`: white and dark red headwraps, an iron cap, an iron cap with a lamellar hood, black
  lacquered lamellar laced in red (armour.leather on this body), iron lamellar, a wide straw hat, the Daoist's tall cap with a
  jade pin and his slate-blue robe, the tall lacquered shield and a round rattan shield. The ji is drawn from shapes in
  gear.json: a spear with a side blade and a red tassel.
- The Han helmets, shield.lacquer and weapon.ji already existed as Han-tagged parts and now have their own gear. New parts,
  `only_if: ["han"]`: armour.han_iron, hat.straw, hat.headwrap, hat.daoist, shield.rattan. New materials: `lacquer_black`,
  `straw`, `slate`.
- voxel_check caught the first body: in the test kit only 43% of the man still showed (at least 45%). A slightly longer, wider
  robe skirt fixed it.
- The first straw hat came out as a tall cone that read as a helmet; flattening it to a wide shallow cone fixed it.
