# 2026-11-13: the Roman kit, round two

Back to the human redesign after the Creel split (#417). This round is #442, three things on the Roman sheet that the
first .vox build (#421, #427) left out.

## What happened
- **The scutum** is now a .vox model (`roman_scutum.vox`, from `tools/vox/roman_gear.lobster`): a tall rectangle bowed
  round the bearer, in team colour, with a bronze rim and spina, a boss, and gold wings out from it. It used to be three
  flat boxes. It's hand gear, so every creature with a scutum gets it.
- **The helmets read apart.** The montefortino is a taller bronze bowl now, with the sheet's black horsehair tail down
  the back (it had a team tuft before, so it looked like the galea's crest). The galea keeps its iron and team crest and
  gets a wider neck guard.
- **The hero has a helmet.** `hero.helm_transverse` went to the generic `hat`, whose `helm` shape doesn't wrap a .vox
  head, so the Roman hero was bare-headed. The new gear `centurion` fits the Roman body with a silvered galea and a team
  crest from ear to ear.

## Funny moments
- The first scutum came out lying on its side: the hand's frame holds gear with its x pointing up.
- The first transverse crest baked about as wide as the galea's fore-and-aft one. Its ends were under a voxel thick, so the bake
  lost them. At 3 voxels thick all the way along, it reads as a hero's crest.

## Later: culture kits (#444)
After #443 merged, I drew the Roman age strip (`human-redesign/romans-ages.png`). It shows the spearman, swordsman and
archer across three ages, each keeping a marker (oval scutum and hasta; rectangular scutum and gladius; cone helmet, beard
and bow) while the kit goes up. Then I checked what the game actually fields for "romans". It was a bone-club "Clubber",
a spearman with no helmet, a pilum-thrower behind a scutum, and a lion-hooded hero: the generator picks gear from tags, so
the sheets never reached a match. Dominic chose culture kits in data (D-249).

- The generator runs as before, then swaps in the kit. That keeps every other line's random draws and every other race the
  same. Golden showed only the imperial races and the starts with Romans in them changing.
- The first try kept the scutum on the pilum-thrower and the galea on the archer. "romans" also puts the tags `roman`
  and `shield` on the brief, so those parts counted as "asked for". Now only a signature tag that none of the culture's
  kits carry counts, so cheese romans still swing their cheese hammer.
- A gladius at age 1 broke "no later kit at age 1", because the gladius is an age-2 part. The kit now leaves an age's
  tiers alone, and the age-1 legionary carries a club under his montefortino.
- `images/2026-11-13/03-roman-kits-by-age.png`: rows are spearman, swordsman and archer; columns are ages 1 to 3.
- `images/2026-11-13/04-roman-kits-in-match.png`: the Roman army in a match at age 1 (montefortinos; plain pilum-throwers
  and archers until their markers come; the Testudo Guard in segmentata).

Still missing from the sheets (next): the sun hat, the pectoral, greaves, the oval scutum, the archer's cone helmet and
beard, the skirmisher's wolf pelt, the healer priest, and the lion hood.

## Later still: the sheet's markers (#448)
Dominic said "continue", so the pieces the kits lacked came next, stacked on #445. These are new Roman parts with their
.vox models:
- the spearman's sun hat, pectoral and oval scutum;
- the archer's cone helmet, whose Roman model carries his beard (gear can bring a face with it);
- the skirmisher's wolf pelt;
- the healer's mantle over the head, with a long white beard;
- the lion hood for the hero who rolls it.

The first wolf, veil and lion read as long brown or blond hair. Each got a colour of its own (new materials `wolf`,
`lion_mane`, `mantle`) and a bigger muzzle or face. The priest's white ankle robe was drawn too, but healers wear nothing
in the torso slot (a run_tests rule), so it waits for a priest body.
