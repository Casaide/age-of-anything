# 2026-11-13: the Roman kit, round two

Back to the human redesign after the Creel split (#417). This round is #442, three things on the Roman sheet that the
first .vox build (#421, #427) left out.

## What happened
- **The scutum** is now a .vox model (`roman_scutum.vox`, from `tools/vox/roman_gear.lobster`): a tall rectangle bowed
  round the bearer, in team colour, with a bronze rim and spina, a boss, and gold wings out from it. It used to be three
  flat boxes. It's hand gear, so every creature with a scutum gets it.
- **The helmets read apart.** The montefortino is a taller bronze bowl now, with the sheet's black horsehair tail down
  the back (it had a team tuft before, so it looked like the galea's crest). The galea keeps its iron and team crest and
  gets a wider neck guard.
- **The hero has a helmet.** `hero.helm_transverse` went to the generic `hat`, whose `helm` shape doesn't wrap a .vox
  head, so the Roman hero was bare-headed. The new gear `centurion` fits the Roman body with a silvered galea and a team
  crest from ear to ear.

## Funny moments
- The first scutum came out lying on its side: the hand's frame holds gear with its x pointing up.
- The first transverse crest baked about as wide as the galea's fore-and-aft one. Its ends were under a voxel thick, so the bake
  lost them. At 3 voxels thick all the way along, it reads as a hero's crest.
