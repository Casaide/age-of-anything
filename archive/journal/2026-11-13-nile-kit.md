# 2026-11-13: The Nile kit

## What happened
- Claude built the fifth culture from the approved sheets (`human-redesign/nile-base.png`, `nile-foot.png`, `nile-riders.png`)
  as the Royal and Steppe kits were built (#467). The Nile body (`nile.vox`, from `tools/vox/nile.lobster`) is on the Roman
  build: a short black wig cut at the brow and the jaw, kohl, a bare chest under a bead collar in blue, gold and red bands, a
  white kilt with a team sash and front panel, bare legs and sandals.
- Gear in `tools/vox/nile_gear.lobster`: the plain khat, the team-striped headcloth with its lappets, the war crown studded with
  gold and a cobra at the brow, the bronze scale corselet (armour.bronze on this body), a red leather cuirass (armour.leather),
  the archer's crossed linen bands, a feather in a team headband, the priest's robe and leopard skin, and two held models: the
  tall cowhide shield and the khopesh.
- The Nile helmets (helm.nile_khat, helm.nile_headcloth, helm.nile_bronze), shield.cowhide and weapon.khopesh already existed
  as Nile-tagged parts; they now have their own gear. New parts, `only_if: ["nile"]`: armour.bands, hat.feather, hat.priest.
  A new material `faience` for the collar's blue.
- The first kilt was mostly team colour from three-quarters: the front panel stood out from the kilt like a slab. A thin panel
  fixed it.

## Decisions
- The cowhide shield and the khopesh are Nile-tagged parts, so giving them their own held models is fine; any other race that
  draws one gets the Nile look with it.
- Healers can't wear torso armour (run_tests), so the priest's robe comes with his head gear, as the monk's habit does.
