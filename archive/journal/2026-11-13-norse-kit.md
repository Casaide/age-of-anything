  iron boss. Mail and bronze armour are drawn as the Royal hauberk on this body.

  and smaller in the crowd. It keeps shield.round and armour.bronze now. test_movement passes 96 of 96.
- test_formations has the known #453 facing check failing 4 times on this branch against 3 on main: the same check, a
  different mix of units. #453 is parked.
