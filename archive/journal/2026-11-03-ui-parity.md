# 2026-11-03: the lobby and HUD checked against the Godot game

## What happened
After the tech tree (which had never been ported), Dominic wanted the rest of the UI checked the same way: the
Lobster lobby and in-match HUD against the Godot one they were ported from, keeping only what later decisions changed.
Claude captured every lobby page and a match, put each beside the nearest Godot picture (`2026-10-12/211-...`,
`2026-10-17/07-norse-lobby-card.png`, `2026-10-13/470-...`, `2026-10-16/15-...` and `17-...`, `2026-10-16/24-...`)
and read the Godot code from git (`22be3d3^`: `scenes/match_setup.gd`, `match_loader.gd`, `sim_debug.gd`,
`src/view/ui/theme.gd`, `ui_materials.gd`, `trait_strip.gd`, `top_bar.gd`, `command_panel.gd`) for the numbers.

Midway Dominic sent a picture of the pause menu: "this looks bad". It went first.

## What had drifted, and what was done
- **The lobby's scale.** Godot drew the lobby at 76% of filling the window (its default UI size, 40%:
  0.6 + 0.4 x 0.4); Lobster filled it. Everything was a third too big, so the side panels were narrow, the centre
  column wide and the armies large. `fe_fit` now scales as Godot did, and the pages lay out to the window's height
  (`fe_h`) rather than a fixed 950. The title had been shrunk by hand to look right at the old scale; it's laid out
  from `_menu()`'s numbers again (crest 90, title 104, buttons 400 x 88 / 62).
- **Type.** Godot's captions were bold, letter-spaced and in the look's metal, lit (theme.gd's `caps_font`): YOU,
  THE AI, BATTLEFIELD, THE AI PLAYS, the trait chips, the panel's ORDERS / BUILD / TRAINING. Lobster had plain or
  display type in the dim colour. A caption face (3) now draws a letter at a time, spaced. Button labels are bold
  (theme.gd's buttons) at the lobby's type; " · " instead of " - " between creature and culture; the top bar's race
  name as typed (the display face sets small capitals; Lobster had it all in capitals).
- **The match loader** was the lobby's look on a wood table; Godot's had its own plain dark one (`loader_style.gd`):
  the names in the text face over their team's stripe with "Rats, Sewer" under them, a stage 46% of the height (300
  to 540), five a side, the map card (picture, BATTLEFIELD, name, size) beside the bar. Redrawn as that.
- **Rules** on the saves and network cards: a fine line with a diamond, not a 2 px bar.
- **The pause menu** (Dominic's picture): a tall slab the height of the screen, "Paused" small in a corner, every
  button the same. Now Godot's card: sized to what's in it at the HUD's scale, the race's frame and crest, the title
  centred in the display face with the race and the map under it and a rule, Resume the primary, then Save, Load,
  the tech tree and the volumes; then, set apart under a second rule and in red, Surrender, Back to the menu and Quit
  (Godot had no Quit and no red group: the coordinator's call, from Dominic's note that nothing read as a hierarchy).
- **The materials.** Two things made faces loud. Godot painted its tiles at twice the UI's pixel size and drew them
  at half; Lobster's shader painted them at the full window scale, so every grain and dimple was 2-3x too big
  (`UI_MAT_PX` 0.4 now). And metal's scratches ran down instead of across (x and y swapped against `_metal`'s field):
  the streaks in Dominic's picture. Its dimples are half as loud too, nearer the sewer look's dark iron in
  `2026-10-12/221-...`.
- **The opening tips** ("Tip 1 of 3: Click your Town Center...") were lost in the port (they were on the 10-29 list
  of what was missing). Back under the top bar, with the pad's words on a pad.
- Lobby captures now wait until the armies' first looks are baked: before, a capture often caught one side half
  drawn (one frog against six bears).

## Intended differences (left as they are)
- Voxel units everywhere (D-201, D-202), so the armies look different and humans stand taller.
- Music and Sound volumes on the title page and in the pause menu (2026-10-29, Dominic: music too loud).
- The HUD at 115% (2026-10-29, Dominic: the port's HUD was too small).
- The prompt box empty with its placeholder on entry (Godot did the same; its pictures had words typed).
- The boot screen (2026-10-30); the zoomed start of a match (2026-10-31); the tech tree page (ported this morning).

## Left
- The Race Lab is still the old style it was in Godot too (m19 left it); "Host a saved game" on the network page and
  the shared network lobby are in the backlog already.
- Cmd + and - don't change the lobby's size (Godot's did): the backlog has it.
- A face's light is a little brighter than Godot's (the shader's cellular noise isn't FastNoiseLite's normalised
  one); close enough to leave unless Dominic minds.
