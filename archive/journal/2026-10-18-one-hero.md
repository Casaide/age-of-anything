# 2026-10-18: One hero, not three

**Milestone:** 14 (race identity and heroes), after its close
**Who:** Dominic + Claude (Claude Code)

## What happened
Dominic checked the named-hero change (D-149) had merged, then saw the Town Center still offering three heroes. That
was the agreed behaviour for a prompt naming none, but not what he wanted: "there should never be 3 heroes to buy".
So every race now gets one hero: the one its prompt names, else one from its pool picked by the race key.

The sim tests had leaned on several heroes a side (two in a queue, two auras not adding up, one falling beside
another); they now train, aura and revive the one. The bot-vs-bot check went from "fields several of its heroes"
to "fields its one".

## Decisions
- D-159: one hero a race; the prompt picks it, or the key does.
