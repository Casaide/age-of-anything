# 2026-10-17: The bears go into play

## What happened
Dominic: "lets get the bears going on a branch". The bears had sat in `data/later/bears.json` since the concept
rounds of 2026-10-13. Dominic had picked the B2 heads ("the snouts look perfect") and kept the B3 mount, and left the
rest to Claude (D-126, D-127), so this session built them in rather than starting another concept round. The work
was built on the `bears` branch and merged into main on 2026-10-18, its decisions renumbered D-159 to D-161
(written as D-160 to D-162 on the branch).

- The data moved into the game's own files: the four kits into `data/creature_kit.json`, the nine parts into
  `data/parts.json`, the three creatures, four skins and the words into theirs. DataLib now registers the kits and
  completes kit bodies on load, the first creature in play made by the kit instead of by hand. The helmets left
  `bear_concepts.gd` for `bear_models.gd`.
- "bears" give a brown bear race, "moonbears" a black one and "polar bears" a polar one. "Viking bears" are
  Norse, "romans riding bears" are humans on bears and "rats riding bears" rats on bears: a word that is both a race
  and a mount becomes the mount once a riding word has come first.
- Bears walk. Nothing small carries them, so their large line is a giant bear and they have no mounted archers.
- A bear voice: a huff when picked, a low "hrrm" for orders, a long falling roar when one dies.
- The first bot match: bears beat sewer rats on Reed Meadow in about 25 minutes. A round robin on Reed Meadow
  (bears, polar bears, sewer rats, imperial humans, both seatings): bears 2 of 6 (33%), sewer rats 2 of 6,
  imperial humans 1 of 6, polar bears 0 of 6, and 7 draws at 35 minutes. The polar bears (forest by roll) are the
  weak ones, for the balance look in the backlog.

## The placement check had the most to say
The placement cases already covered the concept units. Bears in play came to much more: every culture's helmets,
heroes, Norse gear, engines, war beasts. The first run had 102 failures. Claude added a sweep putting every helmet,
armour and held piece on each bear body, which made it 191. All of them were fixed through data (D-160):
- Caps and leaf hats floated above the bears' heads, because the helmet socket sat a unit over the skull (it was
  placed for the ear-cup helmet). It now sits on the skull, and the bears' own helmets are lifted to where they were.
- The cloth cape cut through the broad back. A body can now name a socket for one part, `"cape.cloth"`.
- A banner's crossbar ran through tall helmets: a bear's head is up where the flag flies on a human.
- The Attic helmet's rim came down over every bear's eyes. Bears don't wear it, and they have their own helmets.
- Bear crews didn't fit inside a ram, and two bears on a war elephant's back were inside each other. Bears now work
  engines from beside them, as centaurs do.
- A sitting bear mount raised its head into its rider's lance. Ridden, it only settles back a little.
- The 2D half found another 205 failures: a bear archer standing at ease used a body picture that was never baked
  ("body.bear@draw@ease"). Two-legged bodies with idle poses were new, and no hand-built one rests while holding a
  bow. Bodies now bake each idle pose with the unit's hold. The black bear's shoulders also showed past the siege
  tower's back corner, so the tower gets a place further back for wide crews.
