## Rules (Server Settings → Safety Setup → Rules, and pinned in #rules)

1. **Be decent.** Treat people well. No harassment, hate, slurs or personal attacks. Disagree with the idea, not the
   person.
2. **Keep it safe for work.** No NSFW, gore or shock content, including in usernames, avatars, races and screenshots.
3. **Stay on topic.** Use the channel that fits. Off-topic chat lives in #off-topic.
4. **No spam or self-promotion.** No ads, server invites or unsolicited DMs. Sharing your own mod or video is fine in
   #show-your-army or #modding.
5. **Builds stay private.** Test builds and anything marked private stay in this server. Don't reshare files, keys or
   screenshots from them without asking.
6. **Other people's IP.** Fan races and mods based on other franchises are fine to play with privately. Don't post them
   as official content or ask for them to be added to the game.
7. **Bug reports help most with detail.** Include the build, what you did, what happened and, if you can, the race key
   and a screenshot.
8. **The mods have the final say.** If a mod asks you to stop, stop. Questions about moderation go to a DM with a mod,
   not the channel.

**START HERE**

**THE GAME**

- `#show-your-army` (forum, or a media channel): your prompts, races, armies and battles
- `#ideas` (forum): peoples, gear, words, cultures and features you'd like
- `#bug-reports` (forum): one post per bug
- `#steam-deck`: playing with a pad and on the Deck
- `#modding`: making mods, the data format, help

**OTHER**
- `#off-topic`
- `#voice` (voice channel), for matches together once builds are out

**STAFF** (private): `#mod-log`, `#mod-chat`

### Channel topics (the line under the name, 1024 max)

- `#show-your-army`: Post a race: the prompt, its name and hero, a screenshot. Share its race key so others can face it.
- `#ideas`: One idea per post. Say what you'd type, and what you'd expect to get.
- `#bug-reports`: One bug per post. The build, what you did, what happened, what you expected, a screenshot or the race key.
- `#steam-deck`: Pads, the order ring, Deck settings and how it runs.
- `#modding`: New parts, creatures, cultures and words as data. Ask for help, share what you've made.
