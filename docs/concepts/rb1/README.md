# RB1: the rats' large unit (2026-10-11)

Dominic: the giant rat "is just upsized"; it should be a different kind of rodent. Four options,
modelled as bodies at the large unit's scale, next to today's rat spearman (left).
`rat_large_close.png` is 3x, `rat_large_game.png` is game zoom.

| Option | Reads at game scale? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Capybara "big cousin" cavalry | yes: a brown brick with a blunt head | tea-towel saddle with a team stripe; a rat rider goes on top (mount-first rule) |
| Porcupine pin-fortress | yes: the quill fan is the silhouette | team colour still needed (bottle-cap plates in the quills) |
| Naked mole-rat rammer | no: reads as a pig | incisors too small; needs a longer, lower body and big teeth |
| Sewer brute | half: the long snout over the forearms reads as an elephant's trunk | shorter snout, bigger hands |

Not modelled yet: the Rat King swarm (5-7 rats knotted at the tails).

Claude's pick: the capybara. It plugs into the mount rule, reads at a glance and is funny played
straight. The porcupine is a good second, as a later-age heavy.
