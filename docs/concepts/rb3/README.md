# RB3: the rats' buildings by age (2026-10-11)

From the rats-and-bugs review: their buildings are human templates in junk materials, the house is
too big, and nothing changes with the ages. Rats are proud: by the top age they copy the Romans.

`rat_houses.png`: today's tin-can house, three smaller options, and the stone-culture house for scale.
- **Can on its side**, half sunk, the door in its open end (age 1).
- **Two cans stacked** with a ladder (age 2).
- **A cotton reel** made into a round house, the thread still wound on (age 2 or 3).

`rat_barracks_tc.png`: today's junk barracks, three options, then the town centre now and at age 3.
- **Sardine tin**: the hall is the tin, its lid rolled back on the key.
- **Crate fort**: crates stacked round a yard, a mousetrap as the drill frame.
- **Drainpipe**: a clay pipe sunk in the ground, planked across its mouth with a door.
- **Age-3 town centre ("pride")**: the same hall with a porch of oil-drum columns (two drums each,
  rust red and blue), a pediment cut from a cracked dinner plate, broken crockery paving the front.

Claude's picks: houses can-on-side (age 1) growing into the stack (age 2) and the reel (age 3);
the drainpipe barracks (it sits low in the ground, D-081); the pride town centre for age 3.
Buildings changing with the ages needs engine work (`arch:type:age`), which follows the pick.
