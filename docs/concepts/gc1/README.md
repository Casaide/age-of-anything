# GC1: gear by age for the Nile, Han and Aztec cultures (2026-10-11)

These three cultures came in as data (D-091) wearing the shared kit. This round gives each one
head gear for ages 1, 2 and 3, and a signature weapon and shield (shown together in the last
column). Each culture has a sheet close up (`*_close.png`) and one at game size (`*_game.png`).
The torso armour is left off so the gear reads. Concept ids are in `scenes/art_pixel/concepts.gd`.

| Culture | Age 1 | Age 2 | Age 3 | Weapon | Shield |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nile | linen head bag | striped headcloth with lappets | bronze helmet, ridge and cheek guards | sickle sword | tall round-topped cowhide |
| Han | topknot with a team band | black cap with stiff tails | pointed lamellar helmet, red tassel, neck lames | halberd with a side blade | lacquered red, pointed top |
| Aztec | bowl of hair, headband, one feather | quilted cotton cap, feather crest | eagle helmet (beak over the brow, white crest) | obsidian-edged club | round, feather fringe |
